今天整理了国际工程现场最实用的**电缆敷设英语50句**，建议收藏。

# 一、施工准备阶段

## Preparation Stage

### 1

电缆已经到场了吗？

Has the cable arrived at site?

---

### 2

请检查电缆盘是否完好。

Please check whether the cable drum is intact.

---

### 3

发现电缆盘有损坏。

Damage has been found on the cable drum.

---

### 4

请核对电缆型号。

Please verify the cable type.

---

### 5

请确认电缆长度。

Please confirm the cable length.

---

### 6

电缆规格与设计一致。

The cable specification complies with the design.

---

### 7

电缆沟已经清理完毕。

The cable trench has been cleaned.

---

### 8

请清除沟内积水。

Please remove the standing water from the trench.

---

### 9

电缆路径已经放样。

The cable route has been marked out.

---

### 10

请检查桥架安装情况。

Please inspect the cable tray installation.

# 现场对话实例

## Site Conversation Example

中国工程师：

这根电缆为什么停下来了？

Why has this cable pulling operation stopped?

---

施工队长：

前面弯曲半径太小，需要调整路径。

The bending radius ahead is too small. We need to adjust the route.

---

中国工程师：

调整后再继续敷设。

Continue the cable laying after adjustment.

---

施工队长：

好的，我们马上处理。

Okay, we will handle it immediately.

# 结语

## Conclusion

在国际电力工程项目中，电缆敷设几乎每天都会遇到。与其死记硬背大量单词，不如掌握现场真正高频使用的句子。

记住一个原则：

**学现场英语，不是学考试英语。**

**Learn Site English, Not Exam English.**

当你能够熟练说出：

- Please release the cable slowly.
    
- Check the cable route.
    
- Verify the phase sequence.
    
- The cable laying work has been completed.
    

那么你已经具备了国际工程电缆施工沟通的基本能力。
